# Job Stat Rebalance - Tier 1 & Tier 2

## Overview
Increased stats for Tier 1 and Tier 2 jobs to create a more dramatic power curve. The gap between Tier 1 and Tier 2 is now significantly larger than the gap between Basic and Tier 1.

## Stat Increases

### Tier 1 Jobs: ~30% increase
All Tier 1 job stats increased by approximately 30% to make them feel more impactful.

### Tier 2 Jobs: ~50% increase  
All Tier 2 job stats increased by approximately 50% to create a massive power spike and reward player progression.

## New Progression Curve

### Basic Jobs (Tier 0)
**Average Total Stats: ~4.5**
- Warrior: STR 2.0, TGH 1.5, CON 1.0
- Mage: INT 2.0, CONC 1.5, FRT 1.0
- Rogue: DEX 2.0, AGI 1.5, CRIT_C 2.0, CRIT_D 1.0

## Impact on Gameplay

### Power Progression
- **Basic → Tier 1**: More impactful early-game progression (+~33% increase in power gap)
- **Tier 1 → Tier 2**: Massive mid-to-late game power spike (+~62% increase in power gap)
- Tier 2 jobs now feel like true elite specializations worthy of their difficult unlock requirements

### Balance Considerations
- Specialist jobs (Grand Master, Sage, Apex Predator) remain the most powerful
- Hybrid classes provide strong versatility with high total stats
- Pure specializations (Berserker, Assassin) excel in specific areas
- Tank specializations (Juggernaut, Battle Priest) gain significantly more survivability

### Player Experience
- Earlier unlocking of Tier 1 jobs feels more rewarding
- Tier 2 jobs provide a massive sense of progression achievement
- Late-game builds become significantly more powerful
- Combat effectiveness increases dramatically with tier progression

## Backward Compatibility
These changes affect the stats gained per level for each job. Players with existing saves will immediately benefit from the increased stats based on their current job levels. No save migration required.
